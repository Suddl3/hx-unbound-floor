HX Unbound Floor

Build custom firmware for the Line 6 Helix Floor from your own Line 6 file, and turn .nam captures into files it can play. Everything runs in this browser. Nothing is uploaded.

Unofficial and experimental. Not made or supported by Line 6. Tested on one Helix Floor. Installing modified firmware is at your own risk and may void your warranty. Read Recovery first.

Build firmware

You need a Helix Floor (not LT, Rack, Stomp or Stadium) and Line 6's file Helix_Floor_v3.80.0_0x7d01f5efb.hxf, unchanged. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer.

Always included: two NAM A2 Lite blocks per path, faster DSP code for stock amps and drives (about 15% less DSP per amp measured on one Floor; the block limit is unchanged), HX Edit Unbound support and a fix for a stopped MIDI clock.

How to install it
      
    
        
  1. On the Floor, select a preset without a NAM block.
    2. 
        
  2. Close HX Edit. Open Line 6 Updater, choose Offline Mode, then your Helix Floor.
    3. 
        
  3. Choose File, pick the downloaded file, then Update and Accept. Don't unplug or switch off until it finishes (about 20 minutes).
    4. 
        
  4. The Floor restarts, possibly on a factory setlist: switch back to your own.
    5. 
      

    

    
Please don't share the built file: it is Line 6's firmware with changes. The DSP rewrites are nocaseguy's work (GPL-3.0).

  


  

    
Convert captures

    
Pick .nam A2 Lite captures. Each becomes a .wav to import in HX Edit as a User IR (it carries the capture, not a sound). The files are numbered in slot order. On the Floor, add Amp, NAM, NAM A2 Lite and turn Model to the slot number.

Recovery, or going back to stock

    
    
      
  1. Switch the Floor off. Hold footswitches 6 + 12 and switch it on; keep holding until it shows update mode.
    2. 
      
  2. In Line 6 Updater (Offline Mode), install the stock Helix_Floor_v3.80.0_0x7d01f5efb.hxf.
    3. 
    

    
Your presets and IRs stay on the unit. NAM blocks in presets go silent on stock firmware.
