NAM captures on your Helix Floor

Build NAM A2 Lite firmware from your own Line 6 file, install it, and turn your .nam captures into files the Floor can play. Everything runs in this browser. Nothing is uploaded.

Unofficial and experimental. Not made or supported by Line 6. Tested on one Helix Floor. Installing modified firmware is at your own risk and may void your warranty. Read Recovery first.

3Install it with Line 6 Updater

    
    
      
  1. On the Floor, select a preset without a NAM block. (The Floor starts on the last preset; this way a power cycle always comes back cleanly.)
    2. 
      
  2. Close HX Edit. Open Line 6 Updater, choose Offline Mode, then your Helix Floor.
    3. 
      
  3. Choose File → the file from step 2 → Update → Accept. Don't unplug or switch off until it finishes (about 20 minutes).
    4. 
      
  4. The Floor restarts. If the Updater's last page shows a web error, that's only its info panel; the install is done.
    5. 
      
  5. After any firmware install the Floor starts on a factory setlist: switch back to your own setlist.
    6. 
    

  


  

    
4Convert your captures

    
Pick all your .nam A2 Lite captures at once. Each becomes a .wav that HX Edit imports as a User IR (it carries the capture, not a sound). Choose the first free IR slot: the files are numbered in slot order, so the slot is part of each capture's name.

5Load your captures and play

    
    
      
  1. Unzip the download. In HX Edit, open Impulses, select the first slot from step 4, and import the .wav files in name order. Check each one landed in the slot its name starts with.
    2. 
      
  2. On the Floor, add a block: Amp → NAM → NAM A2 Lite.
    3. 
      
  3. Turn Model to the slot number. The block's title shows the capture's IR name, which starts with its slot number, so you can scroll by name.
    4. 
    

    
The knobs

    

      
ModelWhich capture plays: the User IR slot (1–128) that holds it. A slot with a normal cab IR, or nothing, is silent.

      
InputGain into the capture, −20 to +20 dB; 0 dB at 5.0.

      
Bass / Mid / Treble / PresenceTone stack after the capture, ±12 dB, flat at 5.0.

      
Ch VolOutput level; 0 dB at 5.0.

    

    
How many, and where

    
    
      
  • Up to two NAM blocks per path (Path 1 runs on one DSP, Path 2 on the other): four per preset.
    • 
      
  • One NAM uses about 35% of a DSP. Two on one path leave room for little else, so the layout that works: both NAMs on one path, your effects on the other, with Path 1 feeding Path 2. A stereo rig: Split Y → NAM | NAM → Join.
    • 
      
  • Use a full-rig capture (amp + cab) and you can drop the IR blocks.
    • 
      
  • The Floor refuses a block that would overload a DSP, so a preset can't crackle from too many blocks.
    • 
      
  • Build and edit NAM presets on the Floor, not in HX Edit. HX Edit doesn't know the NAM model and can drop NAM blocks when it rewrites a preset.
    • 
    

  


  

    
Recovery, or going back to stock

    
    
      
  1. Switch the Floor off. Hold footswitches 6 + 12 and switch it on; keep holding until it shows update mode.
    2. 
      
  2. In Line 6 Updater (Offline Mode), install the stock Helix_Floor_v3.80.0_0x7d01f5efb.hxf.
    3. 
    

    
Your presets and IRs stay on the unit. NAM blocks in presets go silent on stock firmware.
